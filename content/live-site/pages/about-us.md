# About Us

ABOUT OUR COMPANY

## Who we are

"Daring to be the First, working hard for a world-class level" has been Sweillem's philosophy and aim since its early establishment. In the heart of Cairo, Egypt, Sweillem started out in 1935 with a small plant manufacturing vitrified clay pipes. Learning, developing, and maintaining the same excellence of quality pushed Sweillem to show its great potentials in serving and adding to the industry of vitrified clay pipes. In 1987, Sweillem constructed a new factory with advanced production lines to meet the demands of the local market at the time. Through time, Sweillem was always keen to keep up with what's new in the field and embracing the intensions for expansion worldwide.

We adopt leading technologies to improve products' quality and to comply with the various specifications and standards in the international markets, leading us to acquire the Egyptian Standards (ES 56/2005), the Saudi Standards (SASO GSO EN 295/2008), the European Standards (EN 295), the German standard (ZPWN 295:2016) ,the American Standards (ASTM-C700), the ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, and OHSAS 18001:2007. Moreover, closely following the markets' demands and deeply understanding the development trend of products, will always be Sweillem's policy to expand its market share worldwide.

Relying on complete R & D facilities and an efficient technical team, made Sweillem the production base of the international markets serving Egypt, Middle East, Far East , and Europe. We have accumulated years of technique and experience in this field and turned it to an international enterprise of superior quality reaching many countries like Germany, Belgium, Holland, Czech, Italy, Poland, Romania, Hungary, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Greece, Singapore, Hong Kong, Brunei, etc.

Shortest delivery time is one of our main objectives. Possessing warehouse facilities in different parts of the world minimizes delivery time to our customers worldwide.

Thus we always dedicate ourselves to pursue a continuous improvement on delivery time, price ratio, and performance of our products.

Our constant will to meet your needs effectively, paying close attention to customer's requests as well as the technology improvement and development, will always lead us to reach the highest market levels and the pioneers of Vitrified Clay Pipes industry worldwide.

## PHYSICAL PROPERTIES

Vitrified Clay Pipes is the highest abrasion resistant material in the sewage lines due to its natural components which are capable to handle a very high velocity of the sewer running through the pipe lines ( up to 10m/s).

## CHEMICAL PROPERTIES

Vitrified Clay pipe is by far the most resistant material to corrosion. It is the only sewer pipe material proven over centuries to be resistant to any sulfide generated acids, most industrial wastes, and solvents or aggressive soils. The decomposition of Sulphur (proteins, Sulphates) forms H2S that is mainly responsible for severe corrosion to the surface of sewer pipes. The ability to repel corrosion increases the life expectancy of vitrified clay pipes more than 100 years.
