# Joint Performance

## Water tightness

Jointing systems C and F must guarantee the following features:

Spigot and socket special design and interference foreseen give a seal that is watertight and a pressure of (0.5, 1 and 2.4 bar) either internal or external. For preventing the penetration of the roots we use a polyurethane joint with hardness of 67 "± 5" shore A. (fig. 1). After filling the pipes with water (laminar flow) and insuring that the air in the line is out, water pressure of (0.5) is applied for a period of 15 minutes. The water loss in this period shouldn't exceed 0.04 liter/ml for the pipeline according to the European Standards.

## Angular Deflection

According to EN 295-3:2012, one pipe in a joint assembly shall be submitted to a deflection in which the joint assembly can resist the test's pressure without any kind of water leakage.
