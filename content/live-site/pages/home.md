# Home

## Hero

**Daring to be the First, working hard for a world-class level**

Sweillem's philosophy and aim since its early establishment.

In the heart of Cairo, Egypt, Sweillem started out in 1935 with a small plant manufacturing vitrified clay pipes. Learning, developing, and maintaining the same excellence of quality pushed Sweillem to show its great potentials in serving and adding to the industry of vitrified clay pipes.

In 1987, Sweillem constructed a new factory with advanced production lines to meet the demands of the local market at the time. Through time, Sweillem was always keen to keep up with what's new in the field and embracing the intensions for expansion worldwide
