# Sustainability

## Sustainability In Action

The Greenhouse Gas Protocol distinguishes between direct emissions from an organization's operations and indirect emissions from its supply chain. Sweillem started calculating its CO₂ footprint in 2023, using that year as a baseline to track progress toward emission reduction goals. The company is committed to declaring emissions categorized into Scope 1 and Scope 2, while still developing Scope 3 calculations.

Figure: https://sweillem.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/GHG-Scopes-.png

### Scope 1:

Figure: https://sweillem.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Chart2.png

"Direct greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from sources that an organization owns or controls" including fuel combustion in vehicles and facility processes. In 2023, Sweillem's Scope 1 emissions totaled 19,963.90 tons of CO₂ (88%), primarily from diesel and natural gas used in production facilities. Since 2020, the company reduced emissions by approximately 5% through switching to natural drying processes.

### Scope 2:

"Indirect GHG emissions resulting from the consumption of purchased electricity, steam, heat, or cooling." In 2023, Sweillem's Scope 2 emissions were 1,464.20 tons of CO₂ location-based (6.5%) and 1,259.20 tons market-based (5.5%), mainly from office and production facility electricity use. Approximately 5% of production facility electricity came from renewable sources in 2023.
